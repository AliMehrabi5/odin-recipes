Burger

Description

A burger is a sandwich made with a cooked patty inside a bun, often served with lettuce, tomato, cheese, and sauces.

Ingredients

  • Hamburger buns
  • Ground beef
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Ketchup or mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Season the ground beef with salt and black pepper.
  2. Shape the beef into burger patties.
  3. Cook the patties in a pan until safely cooked through.
  4. Add cheese to the patties near the end of cooking.
  5. Lightly toast the burger buns.
  6. Place the patties inside the buns.
  7. Add lettuce, tomato, onion, and your favorite sauce.
  8. Serve the burger while warm.
