Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, creamy cheese, and tomato sauce.

It is baked in the oven until the cheese melts and the top becomes golden and delicious.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Chop the onion and garlic, then cook them in olive oil.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Add the tomato sauce, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Place a layer of sauce in a baking dish.
  6. Add pasta sheets, cheese, and more sauce in layers.
  7. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  8. Cover with mozzarella cheese.
  9. Bake for 40–50 minutes until cooked and golden.
  10. Let it rest for a few minutes before serving.
