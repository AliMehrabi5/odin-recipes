Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with dough, tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of toppings.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Oregano
  • Pepperoni or vegetables

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough.
  3. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  4. Add mozzarella cheese and your favorite toppings.
  5. Sprinkle oregano over the pizza.
  6. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden.
  7. Let it cool slightly, slice, and serve.
